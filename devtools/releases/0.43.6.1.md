# Flashback 0.43.6.1

Bug-fix release for Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric).

- Fix rain/snow texture jumps when the camera crosses vertical block boundaries by anchoring texture coordinates to world height.
- Preserve smooth weather-column orientation and edge fading during camera movement.
- Keep weather animation continuous across recorded time corrections and replay seeks.
- Fix high-frequency first-person camera interpolation using source-timed positions and seek-aware timelines, without mutating live entity interpolation during preview.

- Suppress synthetic live-server time updates during replay; recorded time packets remain authoritative.
